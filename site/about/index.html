Research Focus

My research focuses on robot learning for reliable long-horizon manipulation. I investigate how learned skills can remain robust as task contexts change and be composed to accomplish extended tasks. This goal connects my work on skill learning, robot data generation, and human-robot interaction.

I develop methods for learning and composing reusable skills, collecting and synthesizing training data, and enabling people to teach and guide robots. These efforts span task-relevant policy learning, intent communication, and learning safety constraints from demonstrations. Through benchmarks and evaluation systems, I study the capabilities and failure modes of learned policies.
