Hello 👋

I am Yue Yang (杨越),
a Computer Science PhD student at UNC Chapel Hill, with an M.S. in CS from Georgia Tech. I have also done research at Meta Reality Labs Research (RLR) and Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL).

I work on robot learning for reliable long-horizon manipulation, treating a long task as a chain of skills:

  • Collecting and generating the data that teaches skills 
  • Making each skill robust and safe 
  • Chaining skills into long tasks and evaluating them 
Read More

I believe in Slow Science.

Off the clock I photograph birds, who pull off long-horizon tasks with no training data at all :) The results are in my flock.
